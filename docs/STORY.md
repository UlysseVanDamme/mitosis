# The Mitosis story (product and sales)

## Who it is for
The buyer is whoever runs payroll operations at SD Worx: they pay for fewer wrong payroll runs and faster handovers. The daily users are payroll consultants like Sofie, who need an answer they are willing to send to a client, and domain owners like Jan, who want to be interrupted only for decisions that are really theirs. Later, client HR admins get the same trusted answers inside mysdworx.

Why now: in June 2026 SD Worx put about 500 Belgian payroll consultants on AI agents that retrieve knowledge. More AI answers means more answers that sound sure and are wrong. Mitosis sits underneath those agents and tells people which answer to trust.

## The loop we sell
Read, divide, catch the contradiction, ping the owner, decide in one click, fix what depended on it, and give everyone the verified answer. Each pass lowers the team's knowledge debt: the number of open contradictions it still carries. That number is what a manager looks at on Friday.

## The film, in five scenes
1. Monday, 8:40. Sofie inherits the Brouwerij Van Dessel portfolio. A client emails: which index applies in January? She finds three documents and a Teams message, and they give three different answers.
2. Overnight, Mitosis read everything, divided into specialists and caught the problem: a forecast of 2.13%, a final figure of 2.21%, and a company agreement that moves Van Dessel's indexation to 1 February.
3. Jan's phone buzzes. A Slack message from Mitosis: "Two sources disagree about the PC 200 January index. This affects Softwarehuis Delta's payroll run. Final 2.21% (Agoria, December) against forecast 2.13% (Pro-Pay, October)." He confirms 2.21% with one tap.
4. Sofie gets a message: "Your Van Dessel question is verified by Jan Peeters: 2.21% from 1 February, on the base without the brewery premium." She replies to the client.
5. Friday. The PC 200 team's knowledge debt went from 14 open contradictions to 3 this week, and one client payroll config was corrected before the payroll run. (Demo numbers; the real ones come from the eval.)

## Why it is worth paying for
A wrong payroll run is expensive: EY (2022, US data) puts the average cost of fixing one payroll error at $291, and Belgian fines scale with the number of employees affected. Handovers are where knowledge gets lost: in a 2018 Panopto survey (vendor research), 42% of role knowledge was known only to the person doing the job.

## How it spreads
Start with one joint committee (PC 200) and one team. Then all joint committees in Belgium, then the other countries SD Worx serves, then client-facing inside mysdworx. Every verified fact and every owner decision stays in the system, so the ownership map (who decides what) keeps growing, and a competitor starting later does not have it.

## Channels
Owners get conflicts where they already work. The demo posts to Slack through a webhook and also shows the message on a phone mockup in the app. SD Worx's own brief mentions Teams channels, so Teams is the obvious target for a real rollout.
